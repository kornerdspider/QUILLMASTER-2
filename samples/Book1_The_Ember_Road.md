# Chapter 1: The Ferry

Mara stood at the rail while the ferry crossed into Varrow. Mara's eyes were grey in the morning light, and she kept them on the far shore. Tavin was 19 years old and had never left the valley before.

Tavin said the water looked colder than it was. Mara said nothing. In her coat she carried the locked box her mother had left her, and she had not yet found a key that fit it.

***

They reached Varrow at noon. Tavin carried the silver lantern up the hill while Mara asked the dockmaster for directions to the archive.

# Chapter 2: The Archive

Oren was the keeper of Varrow and he did not like visitors. He looked at the locked box for a long moment and then handed it back.

Mara asked him about the silver key. Oren said it had been lost for forty years.

***

That night Tavin walked the archive halls alone. He found a map with the silver key drawn in its corner and took it back to Mara.

# Chapter 3: The Climb

Tavin was 17 years old, and the climb to the high pass made him feel younger still. Mara walked ahead.

At the top they found the silver key in a cairn of stones, and Mara opened the gate to the pass with it.
