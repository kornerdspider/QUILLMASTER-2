Chapter One

A year had passed since the high pass. Mara's eyes were green against the grey sea, and the wind off the water made them water. Tavin was 20 years old now and taller than his father had been.

Tavin said the boat would hold. Mara said it had better.

* * *

They reached Corrin by dusk. Oren was the keeper of Varrow still, and his letter waited for them at the harbour inn.

Chapter Two

Mara turned the locked box over in her hands and set it down again. She had stopped trying keys.

Tavin carried the black compass up to the lighthouse and checked it against the stars.
